# Product — what Zinit is, what it is not, the four products

Keywords: what is Zinit, e-sourcing, eRFX, RFP, GPO, spot buy, agents, scope, not spend analytics,
not procure-to-pay, not ERP.

⚠️ **US framing:** read `product_positioning_us` first — on the US market Zinit is positioned as a
**full-scope sourcing platform**, and tail spend is an entry point, not the definition. The
tail-centric wording below comes from the GTM Playbook v2.0 and is kept for context, not for copy.

## The one-sentence version
Zinit is an AI-native e-sourcing platform. It writes the RFP, finds the suppliers, chases them until
they bid, and compares the bids — across categories, in 30+ languages, live in an hour.

*(Playbook v2.0 wording, tail-centric — superseded for US copy: "…for the indirect and tail spend
that most procurement teams never get around to bidding at all.")*

## The 30-second version (cold call)
Most procurement teams have a policy that says anything over $250,000 goes competitive. In practice
half of it gets bid-waived because nobody has the hours, and everything under that threshold never
sees a bid. We built a tool that takes an RFP from a spreadsheet to live suppliers in minutes instead
of weeks, and there is no subscription — the winning supplier pays a fraction of a percent. Deploy in
an hour, no integration.

## What Zinit is NOT (four boundary confusions that have cost real deal time)
- **Not spend analytics.** Zinit starts where spend analytics ends: source-to-contract, not a spend cube.
- **Not procure-to-pay.** No invoicing, no payment.
- **Not transportation execution.** Does not switch carriers or run freight APIs. A prospect booking a
  call to solve parcel costs is in the wrong meeting — reset scope early.
- **Not a replacement for an ERP or an existing source-to-pay suite.** Position as an orchestration
  layer on top. Enterprise buyers with an established stack make this a hard condition.

## The four products
| Product | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **eRFX platform** | RFP and auction creation from dropped Excel/PDF specs, supplier discovery, multi-round bidding, split awards, bid comparison, CPO dashboard. 30+ languages. | Core. Live in production with enterprise clients. |
| **Zinit GPO** | ~100 pre-negotiated distributor contracts across 9–10 tail categories (MRO, office supplies, uniforms, car rental, small parcel, packaging, propane, waste, construction equipment). Client sends AP data, gets a line-item savings report in 1–2 days. | Live in North America. Free to the client — suppliers pay a rebate on shifted spend. |
| **Maverick / Spot agent** | Employee types what they need into a messenger; the agent scans major marketplaces in ~60 seconds and returns a priced comparison, recording the P-card purchase for visibility. | Live in Southeast Asia. US pilot-only. |
| **Agentic OS** | 12 planned procurement agents, starting with sourcing and supplier discovery. | Roadmap — say so. |

## Honesty rules
- Agentic OS is roadmap. Do not present it as shipped.
- Maverick in the US is pilot-only.
- The platform removes most manual work, but supplier engagement and templates still involve people —
  concede this when a sophisticated buyer raises it.
