# Persona — CPO / VP of Procurement (budget owner, no daily pain)

Titles: CPO, Chief Procurement Officer, VP of Procurement, VP of Sourcing, Head of Procurement,
Supply Chain Director, Head of Supply Chain. Controls budget, does not feel the daily pain.
Keywords: CPO, VP procurement, business case, headcount, cost reduction mandate, roadmap, budget.

## What hurts
Headcount. This persona is often carrying a cost-reduction mandate while a dozen or more people do
PO conversion and vendor onboarding by hand. **They need a business case, not a demo.**

## What sells
A **self-funding story**: indirect savings free up their budget, which funds the rest of their agenda.
Frame the tool as the thing that pays for the roadmap. The supplier-funded commercial model matters
here too — no budget fight, no procurement-software queue.

## What kills it
Looking like a distraction from an existing AI or data roadmap. Position as **additive** to whatever
platform stack they have already committed to — an orchestration layer on top — never as competing
with it or displacing it.

## Framing rules
- ✅ Numbers and mechanism: 12–18% on neglected indirect/tail, 15%+ median; 67% of awards still go to
  incumbents at better prices (nobody has to switch vendors).
- ✅ Deployment in about an hour, no integration required to start — removes the IT-project objection.
- ⚠️ "Do more with less" is acceptable here and with CFOs, unlike with category managers.

## Best-fit outreach angle
Expert-view angle (A1) — peer-level, "comparing notes with sourcing leaders". The hiring angle (A3)
also works but should be framed as *scaling the function*, not as replacing headcount.
