# Play — the GPO wedge (the most important sales mechanic we have)

A low-commitment market-entry tool used to upsell into the eRFX platform. It works because it asks the
client for almost nothing. This is the best "next step" to offer a warm-but-not-committed reply.
Keywords: GPO, benchmark, free, AP data, next step, low commitment, savings report, pilot.

## The mechanic
- The client sends an AP or invoice export with three fields: **supplier name, item description, price**.
- No contract, no integration, no IT review, no cost. Terminable at any time with no penalty.
- Results come back in **one to two business days** as line-item exact matches, not estimates.
- A number lands on the table before anyone has been asked to buy anything.

It has converted several prospects into active relationships, and in at least one case turned a
benchmarking exercise into a signed platform contract. **When a prospect is interested but cannot
commit, this is the ask.**

## Qualify it first
- A client with every major indirect vendor already under contract gets nothing from it.
- A client previously burned by GPO rate creep will be actively hostile to the idea
  (see `objection_savings_and_process`).
- Ask what is already contracted before you offer it.

## Categories covered (~100 pre-negotiated distributor contracts)
MRO, office supplies, uniforms, car rental, small parcel, packaging, propane, waste management,
construction equipment, paint.

## Where this fits in outreach
- **Reply to a warm answer** ("interesting, tell me more") → offer the benchmark, not a demo: it is the
  smallest possible ask and produces a number.
- **Письмо 3 of a sequence** (the value step) → mention the benchmark as a concrete, free next step.
- **Blocked by a competing rollout** → the benchmark needs no system decision, so it survives the block.
