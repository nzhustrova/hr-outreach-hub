# Positioning US — Zinit is a full-scope sourcing platform, not a tail-spend tool

⚠️ **This card overrides the tail-spend framing in every other card.** Confirmed by Stan 2026-08-11:
tail spend may be **one** of the angles or hypotheses, but never the main one. The main promise is
**letting the client put more spend under management**. Older cards were distilled from the GTM
Playbook v2.0, which leads with tail spend — where they conflict, this card wins.

## The one-liner (approved by Stan, 2026-08-11)
> **Zinit unlocks Spend Under Management** — the share of company spend that actually goes through
> competitive sourcing, instead of being passively re-ordered from whoever supplied it last time.

This is the frame. Savings, cycle time and tail spend are all *consequences* of raising that share, and
should be presented that way — not as the product itself.

**Why this framing wins with a CPO.** «Spend under management» is a number they already report upwards
and are already measured on. We are not introducing a new metric to be convinced of; we are moving one
they already own. Savings, by contrast, is a claim they have heard from every vendor.

**What the numbers look like** (investor deck, 11.08 — coverage of spend addressed through competitive
sourcing events):

| Annual spend bracket | Managed today | With Zinit |
|---|---|---|
| > $1M | 40–50% | 100% |
| $250K – $1M | 10–30% | 80–100% |
| < $250K (the tail) | practically none | ~80% |

The bottom row is the tail. It is one row of three — that is exactly its correct weight in the story.
Keywords: positioning, what is Zinit, full scope, sourcing platform, category, direct, indirect,
tail spend, wedge, how to describe the product, one-liner.

## How to describe Zinit in US outreach
**Yes:** an AI-native sourcing platform that runs the whole competitive event — RFP creation from a
dropped spec, supplier discovery, multi-round bidding, split awards, bid comparison, CPO dashboard —
across categories, in 30+ languages, live in an hour with no integration.

**No:** "a tool for the spend you never bid", "tail-spend software", "we handle the long tail".
That is a category the buyer will file under *nice-to-have*, and it caps the conversation at a
fraction of their spend.

## What happens to tail spend then
It stays — but as an **entry point and a proof, not the identity**:
- It is the easiest place to show value fast, because that spend is uncontested and nobody is
  defending it. First event, first number, no political fight.
- It is what the free GPO benchmark measures (see `play_gpo_wedge`) — still a valid first ask.
- The $10K–$250K unbid band remains a strong *opening observation* about their process. Just do not
  let it define what Zinit is.

**Copy rule:** tail spend may appear as the example of where to start, never as the scope of the
product. If the email would still make sense to a CPO who has no tail-spend problem at all, the
framing is right.

## Why this matters for the matrix
Positioning is not a message angle — it is the frame under every angle. A tail-only angle and a
full-scope angle are two different messaging hypotheses and can be tested as separate cells, but the
**default frame for new angles is full scope**.
