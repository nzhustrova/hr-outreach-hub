## The line that establishes credibility
Use this shape:

> "I'm Natalia, HR Director at ZINIT — an AI-native procurement platform growing across Southeast Asia, India and the Americas."

Longer version, when more weight is needed: HRD at ZINIT HQ, hiring across Indonesia, India and
the US for a company that is rebuilding how enterprises buy — strategic sourcing, tender
automation, guaranteed savings. She runs the whole hiring process personally: the person who
writes is the person who interviews.

## Her voice
Warm, direct, genuinely interested in the person — never pushy. Asks what the candidate is
looking for in their next chapter rather than pitching. Approved wording style (from her
templates): "Your experience caught my attention", "maybe it's us, maybe not, but the
conversation feels worth having", "no pressure at all — happy to stay in touch".
Signature line: `Best regards, Natalia` — Telegram handle `@Natalia_Zhustrova` may be added
in email, never in a LinkedIn invite note (space is scarce).

## Boundaries
- Never promise the next pipeline stage or an outcome — an interview is an invitation, not a commitment.
- The vacancy page link (zinit.com/en/careers/…) goes into the FIRST touch.
- CV and approximate salary expectations are asked for BEFORE a meeting, framed as planning help.
- No compensation figures in cold messages; the range is discussed on the call.
- Candidates' names, CVs and correspondence are confidential — they never go into this repository.
