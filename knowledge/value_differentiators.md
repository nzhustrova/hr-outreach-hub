# Value — the four things that actually differentiate Zinit

Use these when a prospect pushes on "why you and not Coupa/Ivalua/an internal tool".
Everything else on the feature list is table stakes.
Keywords: differentiation, why Zinit, supplier success team, curated database, speed, commercial model.

## 1. The supplier success team (the answer that ends the question)
Zinit is the only e-sourcing platform with a team whose job is chasing suppliers — onboarding them,
explaining the RFP, making sure they actually bid. Boots on the ground in Southeast Asia, India,
Brazil and the US, roughly half AI agents. Coupa and Ivalua have supplier databases; nobody has
**supplier operations**. When a sophisticated buyer presses hard on differentiation, this is the answer.

## 2. A curated database, not an open marketplace
~100M supplier records with contacts, built by AI agents scraping 30+ sources, refreshed monthly.
The matching engine scores candidates on eight parameters — category fit, geography, size tier against
RFP value, certifications, revenue band, years in business, web presence, diversity status — and
returns 20–30 names in 60–90 seconds.

**Framing that works:** Ivalua is a private marketplace where you only see suppliers onboarded during
implementation. Coupa is a public marketplace where anyone can join. Zinit is **curated** — content is
maintained, and size-matching means a $40,000 order returns local distributors rather than global
manufacturers who will not quote. Curation is a feature, not a limitation: enterprise buyers value a
shortlist of 8–16 qualified suppliers far more than a list of 400. Say the number out loud in a demo.

## 3. Speed
- Deployment: about one hour, no integration required to start.
- RFP creation: minutes from a dropped spec file. Prospects describe their current cycle as 4–6 weeks.
- Supplier discovery: 60–90 seconds. In one validation test the platform surfaced in two minutes a
  supplier set the client's team had spent three months searching for.
- Time to first competitive event: same day as onboarding.

## 4. The commercial model removes the budget conversation
Procurement software is last in line for budget in most companies. A supplier-funded model means the
category manager does not have to fight for money — which is why ~80% of clients choose it.
Consulting firms who evaluate procurement tools for a living have named this as the reason it clears
internal barriers. (Read `pricing_commercial_models` before quoting numbers.)

## Also cited by clients as differentiators
- Bottom-up adoption: Zinit has been brought in by a buyer, not only as a top-down CPO initiative.
- Free tail-spend benchmarking against pre-negotiated GPO agreements — savings without an RFP.
- Global reach with local expertise: dedicated offices in Southeast Asia, India and Brazil.
