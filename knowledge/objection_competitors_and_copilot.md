# Objections — competitors, Copilot, integration, data security

Answers to "why not X". Every objection here was raised by a real enterprise buyer.
Keywords: Coupa, Ivalua, competitors, Copilot, Microsoft, integration, ERP, source-to-pay, data security, SOC 2.

## "How is this different from Coupa or Ivalua?"
Three points, in this order:
1. **Curation** — Ivalua only shows suppliers onboarded during implementation; Coupa's marketplace lets
   anyone join; Zinit's database is maintained and size-matched to the event.
2. **Supplier operations** — nobody else has a team that gets suppliers to actually bid. *This is the
   point that consistently ends the question.*
3. **Cost** — incumbents run $200K–$1.2M a year, and complaints about their support are common.

## "What does this do that Microsoft Copilot doesn't?" ⚠️ rehearse this one
Copilot can draft an RFP document. It cannot find 20 vetted suppliers with named sales contacts in
90 seconds, it cannot get those suppliers to respond, and it has no bid comparison or award audit
trail. Expect this at every enterprise account with an M365 estate.

## "It has to sit on top of our existing suite. We can't displace it."
Agree immediately and position as an **orchestration layer**. Integration with major source-to-pay and
ERP platforms is estimated at about two weeks. Then propose starting standalone on non-critical
indirect spend — needs no integration and frees capacity without touching the critical path.

## "Our data cannot leave our systems."
SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, penetration test report and cybersecurity insurance available on request.
AI runs in a corporate closed environment with multi-model routing, and can potentially route to the
client's preferred LLM. Data residency and retention are configurable.

## "Anyone can sign up as a supplier. How do you know they're real?"
Suppliers claim an existing record in the database and the supplier success team validates employment
and company information. Each RFP enforces its own pre-qualification — ISO certificates, NDAs,
documentation. A rating system is being built from participation and win history.
**Be honest about the limit:** no site visits, no physical part testing.

## "You say fully autonomous, but we'd still need people for supplier engagement and templates."
They are right. **Concede it.** The honest claim: the platform removes most of the manual work and the
supplier success team absorbs supplier engagement. Overclaiming autonomy to a sophisticated buyer costs
more than it gains.
