# Persona — digital procurement strategist, PE sponsor, consulting partner

Three secondary buyers. Titles: Digital Procurement Lead, Procurement Transformation, Head of
Procurement Technology; PE operating partner; consulting partner / principal.
Keywords: digital procurement, transformation, Copilot, integration, data, PE, portfolio, consulting.

## Digital procurement strategist — technically fluent, skeptical, gatekeeps the evaluation
**What they ask:** what does this give me that Microsoft Copilot doesn't? Does it integrate with our
existing suite? Where does our data go?
**Prepare, don't improvise.** The Copilot question comes up at every enterprise account with an M365
estate — rehearsed answer in `objection_competitors_and_copilot`.
**What sells:** data-security posture (procurement data stays inside the platform, not fed to
general-purpose LLMs), configurable residency/retention, orchestration-layer positioning.

## PE sponsor — buying on behalf of a portfolio, not for themselves
- **What hurts:** supplier onboarding in their incumbent suite is the most consistently cited pain;
  portfolio-company procurement teams are not sophisticated users of enterprise tooling.
- **What they want:** the curated supplier-discovery database, standalone, with a commercial structure
  that fits a fund — not the full suite. Supplier-funded pricing is usually refused; an unlimited
  single-company licence does not fit a fund holding hundreds of portfolio companies.
- **Buying trigger:** exit-readiness — a tool the portfolio company can operate independently.

## Consulting partner — a channel, not a client
- **What hurts:** gain-share engagements with a cycle-time problem; PE clients where speed is the deliverable.
- **What sells:** one named category on a live engagement, this week. Ask which category is
  make-or-break on their current project, then move on it within hours.
- **Watch:** experienced practitioners push back that a mostly client-led support model is too light.
  Fair objection from people who do implementation for a living — describe onboarding support honestly.
- Approved-vendor status inside a major firm's partner network is worth more than individual
  relationships: it produces inbound enterprise introductions.
