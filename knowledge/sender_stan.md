## The line that establishes credibility
**Approved by Stan himself, 2026-08-11.** Use this shape:

> "I'm Stan, co-founder of Zinit. Before this I was one of the leaders at McKinsey's procurement practice."

⛔️ **Do NOT write** "Co-led the procurement practice at McKinsey after graduating HBS" — Stan explicitly
rejected this wording. It overstates the role, and overstating a McKinsey title in front of procurement
executives who know the firm is a fast way to lose the reader.

Longer version, from his own investor-deck bio, when more weight is needed:
7+ years in McKinsey's North America Procurement Practice, leading end-to-end transformations, strategic
sourcing programs and operating model redesigns. MBA from HBS. HBS may be mentioned, but not welded onto
the McKinsey claim as though one caused the other.

Use it at most once, in the first email, and only when the angle needs credibility (the expert-view
angle A1). The hiring angle A3 works without any bio at all — it earns attention with the signal.

## His voice
Practitioner-to-practitioner. Opens from what he sees in other procurement teams
("comparing notes with sourcing leaders at large US enterprises"), asks for the recipient's take
rather than presenting. Consulting background makes the "expert view" framing natural and honest.
