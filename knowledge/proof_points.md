## ⛔️ Naming clients — the rule, confirmed by Stan 2026-08-11
**Never name a client company. Not «with permission», not «a top-10 US bank» if it is identifiable —
never.** Write the profile instead, and a revenue or size figure is allowed:

Size and revenue are what make an anonymous reference credible, so use them — that is the part Stan

## Rules for using proof in cold outreach
- **No client names, ever.** See the rule at the top — profile plus revenue, never the name.
- Prefer the *mechanism* over the *number* in a first touch: a 15% claim from a stranger reads as spam;
  "67% of awards still go to incumbents, at better prices" reads as someone who knows the domain.
- Match the profile to the lead's shape: manufacturer → the MRO multi-plant line; services company →
  the office/packaging line; sceptical enterprise → the multi-billion line.
- Numbers belong in письмо 3 / replies, not in the first cold email (see `objection_` cards for tone).

## Expectation to set early
New-supplier award rate in year one sits **below 30%**, because supplier validation takes time.
Say it up front rather than miss it later.
